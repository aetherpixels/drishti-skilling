Low-Bandwidth Rural Deployment Architecture

Empirical evidence showing how edge-based metadata inference solves the 2G/3G connectivity bottleneck at remote training centres.

TRADITIONAL APPROACH (FAILING)

24/7 Cloud Video Streaming

Continuous raw 1080p RTSP camera streaming from all classroom/lab cameras to cloud servers.

Required Uplink Speed: 1.5 - 2.5 Mbps per camera
Monthly Data Usage: ~45 GB per camera / month
Cloud Storage & GPU Cost: ₹1,800 / camera / month
Rural Viability (2G/3G): 0% (Frequent buffering & dropouts)
❌ Unviable for 15,000+ centres across India; prohibitive cloud compute bills.
DRISHTI-SKILLING (OUR SOLUTION)

Edge-Inference + JSON Telemetry Sync

On-premise edge mini-PC processes frames locally; transmits only lightweight 2-5 KB JSON packets every 5 minutes.

Required Uplink Speed: <0.02 Mbps (2G Friendly)
Monthly Data Usage: ~120 MB per centre / month
Cloud Storage Cost: ₹15 / centre / month
Bandwidth Reduction: 99.4% Reduction!
✔ 100% operational on rural dongles with 72-hour offline store-and-forward caching.

Privacy-Preserving Design Charter (DPDP Act 2023)

What is Identified: Aggregate counts of physical trainees present in the classroom, spatial positions of approved workshop machinery, and classroom illumination level.

What is NOT Identified: Facial biometric vectors, individual names, Aadhaar linkage to video, or gender/demographic classification from video streams.

Edge De-Identification: Irreversible Gaussian blurring applied directly at frame capture in volatile RAM before any storage or cloud transmission.

Tamper-Proof Audit Vault: Snapshot evidence is cryptographically hashed (SHA-256) to ensure legal validity in tribunal reviews without retaining PII.

Complies with Section 6 & 8 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Principles of Purpose Limitation and Data Minimisation).
